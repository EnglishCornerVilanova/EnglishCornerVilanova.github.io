# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** families with children aged 6 to 18 in Vilanova del Camí and the Anoia area, looking for a reliable English school for their child.
- **Secondary:** adults who want to learn English or certify their level.
- **Situation and job:** they are deciding whether to trust this school and want to request a level test or a free trial class. They do it mostly from a phone, by calling or sending a WhatsApp message.

## Product Purpose

The website of English Corner, an English school in Vilanova del Camí (Anoia, Barcelona). It explains what the school teaches and to whom, shows that it is a serious, established school, and turns visits into calls or WhatsApp messages. Success means families and adults contact the school to request a level test or trial class.

## Positioning

- A small, local school in the same premises since 2005, not a chain.
- Cambridge English Preparation Centre.
- A structured method that works on all five areas in every unit (reading, writing, listening, speaking and Use of English), with nothing skipped or improvised.
- Students only move up a level when the teacher decides all five areas are secure, not because a course has ended.
- Stable, qualified teachers who stay year after year.

## Operating Context

- Trilingual site: Catalan is the main language (served at `/`), then Spanish (`/es/`) and British English (`/en/`).
- Contact happens by phone (606 916 483 mobile and WhatsApp; 93 807 07 70 landline) and email; there are no forms.
- Opening hours are read from the school's Google Business listing at build time.
- The latest Instagram posts are read at build time from a Behold feed (free plan, 6 posts).
- The site is rebuilt daily and published on GitHub Pages; it is only published when the owner asks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site generated by a custom Python builder (`build.py`, `src/template.html`, `content/{lang}.json`, `site.json`).
- No forms, no analytics, no advertising or tracking cookies. Google Maps loads only after explicit consent; fonts are self-hosted.
- No group timetables on the website (decided by the owner).
- English copy must be British English at Cambridge Proficiency level, faithful to the Catalan meaning.
- The public GitHub repository must never contain `docs/`, `.env` or private email addresses.

## Brand Commitments

- It is a **school** (academy), never a "shop": the photos show the school's entrance and window.
- Existing logo: Union Jack with "English Corner" in a typewriter face; round London-scene brand mark for the navigation bar.
- Photos are always shown whole, never cropped.
- Official Cambridge names: A2 Key, B1 Preliminary, B2 First, C1 Advanced; Young Learners: Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers, A2 Flyers.

## Product Principles

1. Contact comes first: calling or messaging must always be one tap away on a phone.
2. Show the real school: real photos, real Instagram, real Cambridge credentials, never invented proof.
3. Serious and structured, but warm and local: the tone of a school families trust with their children.
4. Every language is a first-class version, not a translation afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1, touch targets at least 24×24 px, full keyboard access.
- Honour reduced-motion preferences with gentle fades instead of movement.
- Text must survive being enlarged to 200 % on a phone without horizontal scrolling.
